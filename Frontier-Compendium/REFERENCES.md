## 10. Citation rule

- Link to the direct primary or authoritative source where possible.
- Record access or review date for changing specifications.
- Do not cite a neighbouring field as confirmation of an AURA mechanism.
- Preserve contrary and limiting results.
- Very recent preprints remain provisional.
- External references can establish that a problem exists without establishing that AURA solves it.

**Reference principle:** A citation is a bridge to another source, not borrowed certainty.
