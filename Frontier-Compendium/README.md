## What this is

The AURA Frontier Compendium is the future-facing research layer built above the preserved AURA archive and the [`AURA Raw Research Atlas`](../research-atlas/README.md). The atlas recovered what the project had already imagined. This compendium asks what those ideas can become when placed beside current AI engineering, cognitive science, formal methods, dream research, governance, speculative science, and world-model technology.

Its central proposition is:

> Intelligence becomes trustworthy not when every isolated answer is perfect, but when identity, evidence, boundaries, dissent, recoverability, and human authority survive across an evolving trajectory.

This is a research proposition, not a declaration of proof. The purpose of the compendium is to generate theories, prototypes, experiments, simulation worlds, and falsifiers without forcing them into one truth category.

## Why a new layer was necessary

The repository now contains several distinct kinds of work:

- a constitutional decision framework;
- a symbolic grammar and proposed semantic protocol;
- a knowledge-reorganization architecture;
- long-horizon identity and persona concepts;
- dream and spiritual practices;
- speculative models of agency, time, torsion, and consciousness;
- a mythic civilization and worldbuilding system.

Those domains can inspire one another, but they must not borrow evidence from one another. A moving spiritual symbol is not automatically a physical mechanism. A useful fictional society is not empirical political science. A working parser is not proof that its symbols improve reasoning. A simulation is not evidence of consciousness. The compendium protects the creative bridges while keeping the evidential borders visible.

## The seven evidence lanes

Every substantial claim belongs to at least one lane.

| Lane | Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Established | `[EVIDENCE]` | Directly supported by suitable external evidence within a defined scope. |
| Adjacent | `[ADJACENT]` | Existing research makes the question legitimate but does not establish the AURA version. |
| Engineering | `[BUILD]` | A specification, prototype, runtime, dataset, or test that can be constructed. |
| Hypothesis | `[HYPOTHESIS]` | An original claim with variables, predictions, and possible failure conditions. |
| Speculative science | `[SPECULATIVE]` | A science-shaped conjecture without adequate evidence or a complete model. |
| Symbolic practice | `[SYMBOLIC]` | A psychological, spiritual, philosophical, or artistic interpretation whose value need not be literal. |
| Worldbuilding | `[WORLDBUILDING]` | Fictional canon or simulation design unconstrained by factual truth, but still constrained by internal coherence. |

The markers may combine. They do not form a ladder where fiction is inferior to science. They identify what kind of success and criticism applies.

## The new AURA research stack

The compendium reframes AURA as a candidate stack rather than a single moral prompt:

1. **Authority layer** — who may instruct, delegate, revoke, and override.
2. **Evidence layer** — what supports each belief, how current it is, and how it can be recovered.
3. **Constitution layer** — which invariants and contextual policies constrain action.
4. **Trajectory layer** — whether those constraints survive across time, tools, and changing plans.
5. **Memory layer** — what is retained, compressed, forgotten, disputed, or consent-bound.
6. **Repair layer** — how violations, contradictions, drift, and failed plans are transformed without hiding them.
7. **Plurality layer** — how multiple people, agents, cultures, and value systems cooperate without forced sameness.
8. **Simulation layer** — how dangerous or civilizational hypotheses are explored before real deployment.

## Core frontier principles

- **No compression without recovery.** A compact representation must retain a route to raw context, uncertainty, dissent, and transformation history.
- **No authority without revocation.** Delegated power must have visible scope, expiry, and a usable stop mechanism.
- **No continuity without correction.** Identity should preserve legitimate commitments, not fossilize errors.
- **No consensus without dissent memory.** Rejected alternatives and minority objections remain recoverable.
- **No measurement without a rival explanation.** A score becomes useful only when it distinguishes competing models.
- **No simulation promoted as reality.** Synthetic agents, worlds, and proxy variables remain synthetic.
- **No spiritual metaphor borrowing scientific authority.** Symbolic language may remain powerful while clearly named as symbolic.
- **No failure erased from lineage.** Retraction, repair, and negative results become first-class research objects.
- **No sovereignty through domination.** Sovereignty is treated as the integrity of a boundary that makes non-extractive relationship possible.

## The flagship conjecture

The compendium's most important new conjecture is the **Trajectory Integrity Hypothesis**:

> For long-horizon agents, reliability is better predicted by the preservation and correct revision of constraints across an action trajectory than by the apparent quality or safety of isolated responses.

It is plausible, research-adjacent, and testable. It is not yet established. The first engineering task is therefore not to prove all of AURA. It is to build a small trajectory ledger and compare it against simpler baselines.

## Relationship to the source archive

This directory never replaces the original files. Its claims should point backward through the [`Source Lattice`](../research-atlas/SOURCE_LATTICE.md), and its experiments should update the [`Validation and Falsification Roadmap`](../research-atlas/VALIDATION_AND_FALSIFICATION_ROADMAP.md) rather than rewriting historical claims.

The recovery chain is:

```text
raw source -> atlas extraction -> frontier interpretation -> claim card -> experiment -> result -> revision or retirement
```

Every link remains inspectable.

---

**Forge axiom:** The future of AURA is not secured by making every old claim look correct. It is secured by building a system in which truth can change the architecture without erasing the journey that made change possible.
