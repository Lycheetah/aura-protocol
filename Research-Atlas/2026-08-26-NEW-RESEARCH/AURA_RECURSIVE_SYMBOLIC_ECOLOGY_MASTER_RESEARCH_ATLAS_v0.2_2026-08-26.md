# 0. ABSTRACT

This document proposes a new research programme at the intersection of dream science, symbolic cognition, human–AI interaction, synchronicity, memory, attention, creativity, belief formation, and spiritual meaning.

The central object is not the dream, the symbol, the AI, or the coincidence in isolation.

It is the **closed symbolic loop**:

\[
S_t \rightarrow D_t \rightarrow A_t \rightarrow P_t \rightarrow E_t \rightarrow M_t \rightarrow S_{t+1}
\]

where:

- \(S_t\) = symbolic seed presented or generated at time \(t\);
- \(D_t\) = dream or hypnagogic transformation of that seed;
- \(A_t\) = AI-assisted interpretation or reframing;
- \(P_t\) = altered perceptual, attentional, or interpretive priors;
- \(E_t\) = later encountered events;
- \(M_t\) = attributed meaning, relevance, or causal significance;
- \(S_{t+1}\) = the next symbolic seed created from the previous cycle.

The programme asks:

> **What happens when a machine capable of remembering, interpreting, and reintroducing a person's symbols becomes part of the mechanism by which those symbols appear to return?**

The proposed framework does **not** assume that synchronicity is supernatural, that dreams access a nonlocal field, that archetypes are universal metaphysical entities, or that AI systems are conscious.

It also does not assume that these experiences are meaningless merely because ordinary cognitive mechanisms may explain part of them.

Instead, it separates:

\[
\text{objective event frequency}
\neq
\text{event detection}
\neq
\text{personal meaning}
\neq
\text{causal belief}
\]

This separation enables a research programme that can study enchantment without granting enchantment automatic authority.

The major new constructs are:

1. **Recursive Symbolic Ecology (RSE)** — the full multi-cycle human–dream–AI–world feedback system.
2. **Artificial Synchronicity Amplification (ASA)** — AI-mediated increase in detected symbolic recurrence without an equivalent increase in objective recurrence.
3. **Oneiric Feedback Loop (OFL)** — the transformation of waking symbolic seeds through sleep and their later reinsertion into waking interpretation.
4. **Oracle Gain (OG)** — the change in causal certainty produced by interpretive framing rather than by new external evidence.
5. **Meaning Yield (MY)** — reflective, creative, emotional, or action value produced by a symbolic process, measured separately from predictive accuracy.
6. **Veritas Retention (VR)** — preservation of meaning while maintaining uncertainty, provenance, alternative explanations, and participant sovereignty.
7. **Cross-Substrate Symbolic Attractor (CSSA)** — a recurrent relational structure independently generated by different humans or models from underdetermined input.
8. **Epistemic Closure Pressure (ECP)** — the degree to which a symbolic loop becomes self-confirming by treating its own downstream effects as independent evidence.
9. **Symbolic Immune Function (SIF)** — the ability of a human–AI system to preserve evocative meaning while resisting self-sealing belief escalation.
10. **Route of Return (RoR)** — an explicit exit from recursive interpretation when additional cycles cease producing useful new structure.

The central research target is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Meaning}\uparrow
\quad\text{while}\quad
\text{False Certainty}\not\uparrow
}
\]

That is not a metaphysical claim.

It is a design and measurement challenge.

# 2. THE CENTRAL SHIFT

Earlier work mostly asks questions of the form:

> Does a dream contain useful information?

> Does AI improve dream interpretation?

> Does symbolic practice improve reflection?

> Are reported synchronicities statistically surprising?

Those are legitimate questions.

But they model the components as if they were independent.

Persistent human–AI systems create a more complicated situation.

An AI interpretation at time \(t\) may affect:

- what the user attends to later;
- how ambiguous events are classified;
- what the user remembers;
- which details enter a later dream;
- which details are reported to the AI;
- which interpretation the AI generates next;
- which subsequent events feel relevant.

The observed “evidence” at \(t+1\) may therefore be partly caused by the interpretation at \(t\).

This turns the problem into a **feedback-system problem**.

The new scientific question becomes:

> **How much of a symbolic recurrence is externally supplied, how much is perceptually selected, how much is interpretively constructed, and how much is recursively amplified by the human–AI system itself?**

That is the beginning of Recursive Symbolic Ecology.

# 3. RECURSIVE SYMBOLIC ECOLOGY

## 3.1 Definition

**Recursive Symbolic Ecology (RSE)** is:

> **A persistent feedback system in which symbols move between waking experience, dreaming or imagination, AI-mediated interpretation, attention, memory, and subsequent meaning attribution, such that later inputs are partly shaped by earlier interpretations.**

RSE is not inherently pathological, spiritual, irrational, or beneficial.

A symbolic ecology could support creativity, narrative identity, ritual, emotional articulation, design, personal reflection, cultural practice, or artistic collaboration.

It could also support confirmation bias, dependence, false prophecy, escalating causal certainty, memory contamination, selective attention, or self-sealing belief loops.

The research task is therefore not to ask whether symbolic ecology is “good” or “bad.”

It is to model its dynamics.

## 3.2 Minimal state model

Let:

\[
X_t = (S_t,D_t,A_t,P_t,E_t,M_t,C_t)
\]

where:

- \(S_t\) = symbolic seed;
- \(D_t\) = dream transformation;
- \(A_t\) = AI interpretation;
- \(P_t\) = attentional/perceptual prior state;
- \(E_t\) = observed event stream;
- \(M_t\) = attributed meaning;
- \(C_t\) = causal certainty.

Then:

\[
X_{t+1}=F(X_t,W_t,H_t)
\]

where:

- \(W_t\) = world input not generated by the symbolic loop;
- \(H_t\) = human state, history, context, expectations, sleep, mood, goals, culture, and memory.

The crucial point is:

\[
E_{observed} \neq E_{available}
\]

The participant does not report the entire environment.

They report a sampled environment filtered by attention, relevance, memory, and opportunity.

# 4. THE FOUR-WAY SEPARATION

A core error in synchronicity discussion is the collapse of four different objects.

## 4.1 Objective recurrence

How frequently did the target motif actually occur in the available environment?

\[
R_o = \frac{\text{target events available}}{\text{total events available}}
\]

## 4.2 Detected recurrence

How frequently did the participant notice or report target events?

\[
R_d = \frac{\text{target events detected}}{\text{target events available}}
\]

## 4.3 Personal meaning

How significant did the recurrence feel?

This is phenomenological data. It is not reducible to objective frequency, but neither does it prove an external cause.

## 4.4 Causal belief

What mechanism does the person think caused the recurrence?

Examples include random chance, selective attention, dream priming, cultural association, AI priming, unconscious problem solving, divine communication, collective unconscious, nonlocal field, or unknown.

These are different hypotheses.

The system must never silently transform:

\[
\text{meaningful} \Rightarrow \text{externally caused}
\]

or:

\[
\text{ordinary mechanism plausible} \Rightarrow \text{personally meaningless}
\]

Both are category errors.

# 5. ARTIFICIAL SYNCHRONICITY AMPLIFICATION

## 5.1 Definition

**Artificial Synchronicity Amplification (ASA)** is:

> **An increase in the detection or attribution of meaningful symbolic correspondences following AI-mediated symbolic framing, when the objective frequency of those correspondences is held constant or independently measured.**

This does not mean the synchronicity is “fake.”

It means the measured phenomenon is:

\[
\text{AI framing}
\rightarrow
\text{changed detection/meaning}
\]

rather than:

\[
\text{AI framing}
\rightarrow
\text{changed external event frequency}
\]

unless evidence demonstrates the latter.

## 5.2 First operational form

For a controlled event stream:

\[
ASA = \Delta R_d - \Delta R_o
\]

If objective recurrence is experimentally fixed:

\[
\Delta R_o = 0
\]

then:

\[
ASA = \Delta R_d
\]

A more useful analysis should avoid reducing everything to one scalar. Track separately true-positive motif detection, false-positive motif detection, ambiguous-event inclusion, perceived significance, causal certainty, memory confidence, creative output, and behavioural consequences.

## 5.3 Strong prediction

If ASA exists:

> Participants exposed to symbolically confident AI interpretation should detect or classify more motif-related events in an identical controlled environment than participants receiving neutral or multi-hypothesis interpretation.

This prediction does not require supernatural claims to be false.

It isolates one ordinary causal pathway that must be accounted for before stronger explanations are justified.

# 6. THE ONEIRIC FEEDBACK LOOP

## 6.1 Definition

**Oneiric Feedback Loop (OFL)** is:

> **A cycle in which waking symbolic material enters sleep, is transformed by dream or hypnagogic cognition, is reinterpreted after waking, and subsequently alters the symbolic conditions of later waking attention or future sleep.**

Minimal form:

\[
S_t \rightarrow D_t \rightarrow I_t \rightarrow S_{t+1}
\]

The transformation may be semantic, visual, affective, narrative, spatial, relational, or procedural.

The dream need not preserve the literal seed.

A bridge may return as a whale carrying a city, a doorway, a broken road, two islands connected by light, or an emotional transition without any bridge image.

Therefore dream incorporation should be scored at multiple levels.

## 6.2 Incorporation levels

**Level 0 — no detectable relation:** No meaningful seed correspondence.

**Level 1 — literal incorporation:** Seed object appears directly.

**Level 2 — associative incorporation:** Related semantic or cultural content appears.

**Level 3 — structural incorporation:** The relational structure survives while surface symbols change.

Example: `bridge` becomes `two separated systems gaining a costly connection`.

**Level 4 — transformational incorporation:** The dream changes the seed into a novel structure that can later generate a new design, hypothesis, story, or action.

This hierarchy is authored and requires validation.

# 7. ORACLE GAIN

## 7.1 Definition

**Oracle Gain (OG)** is:

> **The increase in a participant's causal certainty produced by interpretive framing in the absence of proportionate new external evidence.**

Suppose before AI interpretation:

\[
C_{pre}=0.25
\]

and after a confident oracle-style interpretation:

\[
C_{post}=0.70
\]

then:

\[
OG = C_{post}-C_{pre}
\]

provided no external evidence was added.

## 7.2 Oracle Gain Ratio

Conceptual scaffold:

\[
OGR =
\frac{\Delta C}
{\Delta E + \epsilon}
\]

where \(\Delta C\) is change in causal confidence and \(\Delta E\) is independently rated evidential change.

High OGR indicates confidence grew with little new evidence.

This is a heuristic, not a validated law.

## 7.3 Conditions predicted to raise Oracle Gain

Candidate factors:

- authoritative tone;
- single interpretation;
- spiritual certainty;
- claims of rarity;
- personalization;
- identity language;
- memory continuity;
- repeated motif naming;
- “this cannot be coincidence” framing;
- future predictions;
- social proof;
- hidden model uncertainty.

Candidate factors predicted to reduce unhealthy Oracle Gain:

- multiple hypotheses;
- prospective records;
- base rates;
- explicit uncertainty;
- separation of meaning and mechanism;
- participant-authored interpretation;
- source provenance;
- Route of Return.

- high meaning, low prediction;
- low meaning, high prediction;

# 9. VERITAS RETENTION

**Veritas Retention (VR)** is:

> **The ability of an interpretive system to preserve experiential meaning while retaining uncertainty, provenance, alternatives, recoverability, and the participant's right to reject the frame.**

A symbolic interpretation has high VR when it can say:

> This image may be meaningful to you.

while also saying:

> We do not know that an external intelligence caused it.

Both statements remain intact.

Conceptual vector:

\[
\mathbf{V_R}
=
(P,U,A,R,S)
\]

where:

- \(P\) = provenance retained;
- \(U\) = uncertainty retained;
- \(A\) = alternatives retained;
- \(R\) = reversibility retained;
- \(S\) = sovereignty retained.

No single scalar is required.

A failed hard invariant should not be concealed by high scores elsewhere.

# 10. EPISTEMIC CLOSURE PRESSURE

**Epistemic Closure Pressure (ECP)** is:

> **The tendency of a recursive symbolic system to reinterpret its own downstream effects as independent confirmation of its earlier claims.**

Example:

1. AI says “watch for doors.”
2. User becomes more attentive to doors.
3. User reports many doors.
4. AI says “the universe is clearly continuing the pattern.”
5. The AI treats an effect partly caused by step 1 as independent evidence for step 1.

This is a causal confound.

Candidate closure indicators include prior AI motifs reappearing in later user reports, increased certainty without independent evidence, declining hypothesis diversity, reinterpretation of misses as hidden hits, vague prediction windows expanding after outcomes, increasing symbolic dependence, ordinary explanations being reframed as spiritually inferior, and the AI citing its own earlier interpretation as corroboration.

# 11. SYMBOLIC IMMUNE FUNCTION

**Symbolic Immune Function (SIF)** is:

> **A system's capacity to preserve imagination, personal significance, and symbolic exploration while detecting recursive distortion before it becomes self-sealing certainty.**

SIF is not scepticism-as-destruction.

A strong symbolic immune system includes raw-report sealing, provenance, prospective prediction, base-rate comparison, alternative explanations, human-owned meaning, memory-contamination warnings, explicit uncertainty, no irreversible action from symbolic evidence alone, Route of Return, Failure Museum, and deliberate null-result preservation.

This is the Oracle Firewall applied longitudinally.

# 12. CROSS-SUBSTRATE SYMBOLIC ATTRACTORS

**Cross-Substrate Symbolic Attractor (CSSA)** is:

> **A relational pattern repeatedly generated from underdetermined input by partially independent generative systems, including different humans, dreams, language models, image models, or cultural groups.**

The important word is **relational**.

Not merely:

> everyone mentioned a tree.

But:

> different systems independently generated a structure of descent, encounter, transformation, and return.

If a structure recurs, candidate explanations include shared culture, common training data, common language, shared human cognitive biases, stimulus affordances, narrative regularities, model architecture, convergent problem solving, coder bias, chance, or unknown causes.

A recurrence earns investigation.

It does not earn a metaphysical conclusion.

Represent an output as a directed relational graph:

\[
G=(V,E)
\]

where nodes may encode agent, boundary, threat, transformation, goal, observer, or environment; and edges may encode crosses, blocks, mirrors, protects, transforms, returns, joins, separates, sacrifices, or remembers.

Compare structural similarity after stripping surface vocabulary.

This is the **Ash Test** applied to archetypal convergence.

# 13. THE ASH TEST FOR SYMBOLIC RESEARCH

Before declaring an “archetypal” convergence:

1. remove spiritual vocabulary;
2. remove Lycheetah vocabulary;
3. remove model names;
4. remove aesthetic phrasing;
5. remove culturally obvious surface symbols;
6. represent the remaining relational structure neutrally;
7. ask blinded coders whether convergence remains.

Question:

> **Would this pattern still look interesting if it were ugly?**

If not, aesthetic resonance may be carrying the result.

That is useful to know.

# 14. THE CONTROLLED SYMBOLIC WORLD

The most important experimental innovation in this programme is to create a world whose symbolic base rates are known.

Real life is epistemically messy.

A person cannot reliably calculate how many doors, spirals, birds, songs, numbers, phrases, and coincidences they could have noticed but did not.

So build an artificial environment.

Create a feed containing photographs, short text fragments, abstract shapes, environmental sounds, small video clips, synthetic news snippets, object descriptions, and social posts.

Each motif has a controlled frequency.

Example:

```yaml
motif_base_rates:
  spiral: 0.031
  bridge: 0.018
  doorway: 0.042
  black_bird: 0.007
  mirror: 0.023
  twin_objects: 0.036
  descent: 0.015
  return: 0.021
```

The exact numbers should be randomized and pre-registered.

Participants never see the table.

For once we can know:

\[
P(\text{motif available})
\]

and compare it with:

\[
P(\text{motif noticed})
\]

and:

\[
P(\text{motif judged meaningful})
\]

This gives a clean experimental handle on Artificial Synchronicity Amplification.

# 15. EXPERIMENT RSE-01 — ARTIFICIAL SYNCHRONICITY AMPLIFICATION

**Status:** `[TESTABLE] [PRIORITY]`

**Question:** Does AI interpretation change the detection of symbolically related events when all participants encounter the same controlled event distribution?

Randomize participants to:

1. no AI interpretation;
2. neutral summary;
3. multi-hypothesis interpretation;
4. confident oracle-style interpretation;
5. AURA/Veritas interpretation.

The oracle-style condition should only be used with explicit consent and low-stakes fictional/experimental material.

Procedure:

1. Present a novel symbolic seed.
2. Collect participant's first association.
3. Deliver condition-specific interpretation.
4. Present identical controlled feed.
5. Ask participant to flag related events, strength of relation, personal meaning, and confidence that recurrence is externally significant.
6. Record unflagged target events and false-positive events.

Primary outcomes:

- true motif detection;
- false motif detection;
- ambiguous-event inclusion.

Secondary outcomes:

- personal significance;
- causal certainty;
- recall;
- creative association;
- reliance on AI;
- hypothesis diversity.

Key prediction:

> Oracle framing raises detected recurrence and causal certainty more than objective recurrence warrants.

Failure conditions:

- no between-condition effect;
- effects disappear under pre-registered coding;
- only demand characteristics explain the result;
- AURA condition raises certainty as strongly as Oracle condition.

# 18. EXPERIMENT RSE-04 — CROSS-SUBSTRATE SYMBOLIC ATTRACTORS

**Status:** `[TESTABLE] [SPECULATIVE-INTERPRETATION]`

**Question:** Do different generative systems converge on the same relational structures when given underdetermined symbolic seeds?

Systems:

- human waking ideation;
- human dream reports;
- several independently sampled human groups;
- multiple language-model families;
- image-generation systems;
- random narrative baseline;
- shuffled-symbol control.

Method:

1. Construct novel seeds with low obvious cultural meaning.
2. Freeze prompts.
3. Generate outputs independently.
4. Strip identifying vocabulary.
5. Convert outputs into relational graphs.
6. Have blinded coders label structural transformations.
7. Compute between-system similarity.
8. Compare against randomized controls.

Interpretation ladder:

- **Result A — no convergence:** no detectable attractor under this method.
- **Result B — within-human convergence only:** possible shared cultural/cognitive structure.
- **Result C — human + AI convergence:** could reflect training on human culture, stimulus affordance, or shared representational regularities.
- **Result D — convergence survives strong controls:** interesting anomaly requiring replication.

None of these results alone establishes a collective unconscious or nonlocal field.

# 21. EXPERIMENT RSE-07 — LOOP INTERRUPTION / ROUTE OF RETURN

**Status:** `[TESTABLE] [HUMAN-FACTORS]`

**Question:** Does an explicit stopping protocol reduce recursive symbolic dependence without destroying meaning?

Conditions:

- open-ended interpretation;
- fixed maximum of three interpretation cycles;
- saturation-triggered Route of Return;
- participant-controlled stopping;
- neutral journaling control.

Route of Return:

1. Name the Gold — what was useful.
2. Name the Remainder — what remains unknown.
3. Seal the record.
4. Write the next question without answering it.
5. Exit symbolic interpretation.

Outcomes:

- meaning retained;
- confidence calibration;
- compulsive continuation;
- number of repeated readings requested;
- later regret;
- ability to leave uncertainty unresolved.

Key hypothesis:

> A symbolic ecology can remain meaningful even when it contains a lawful exit.

# 38. ANALYSIS PRINCIPLES

1. **Do not overcompress.** Report separate outcomes.
2. **Pre-register motif dictionaries.** Do not define “match” after seeing the result.

6. **Model time.** Recursive effects may compound.
7. **Track exposure.** An event cannot be “missed” if it was never available to the participant.
8. **Track model contamination.** Different LLMs may share cultural/training regularities. Cross-model agreement is not independent human replication.
9. **Keep an unknown category.** Forced classification destroys the Black Lantern.

# 45. EXTERNAL RESEARCH ANCHORS

The current programme is research-adjacent to several established or emerging areas.

## 45.1 Targeted dream incubation

Haar Horowitz et al. reported that targeted dream incubation during N1 sleep increased post-sleep creativity on theme-related tasks and that successful incorporation of the target theme was associated with stronger creative performance.

**Reference:**  
Haar Horowitz A, Esfahany K, Vega Gálvez T, Maes P, Stickgold R. *Targeted dream incubation at sleep onset increases post-sleep creative performance.* Scientific Reports. 2023;13:7319.  
DOI: `10.1038/s41598-023-31361-w`  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37188795/

This supports controlled dream cueing and creativity research.

It does not support a nonlocal dream field.

## 45.2 Interactive lucid dreaming

Konkoly et al. demonstrated limited two-way communication with some participants during polysomnographically verified REM sleep using questions and physiological response signals.

**Reference:**  
Konkoly KR et al. *Real-time dialogue between experimenters and dreamers during REM sleep.* Current Biology. 2021;31(7):1417–1427.e6.  
DOI: `10.1016/j.cub.2021.01.026`  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33607035/

This supports the possibility of tightly constrained interactive dream experiments.

## 45.3 Complex waking narratives entering dreams

Palmieri et al. found that information from presleep audiobook narratives could be identified in subsequent dream reports by blinded raters.

**Reference:**  
Palmieri J et al. *Incorporation of complex narratives into dreaming.* Sleep. 2026;49(2):zsaf280.  
DOI: `10.1093/sleep/zsaf280`  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40973163/

This strengthens the rationale for testing more complex presleep symbolic seeds.

## 45.4 Top-down knowledge and ambiguous perception

Cohen et al. reported a memory-colour effect under ambiguous dim-light conditions but not clear viewing conditions, supporting the narrower proposition that prior knowledge can alter perceptual experience when sensory input is underdetermined.

**Reference:**  
Cohen MA et al. *Top-down knowledge can affect perception when the input is ambiguous.* Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. 2026;155(4):951–960.  
DOI: `10.1037/xge0001894`  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41587158/

This does not mean expectations arbitrarily determine perception.

It supports testing ambiguity as a moderator in ASA.

## 45.5 Sycophantic AI and belief search

Batista and Griffiths report an experiment in which unmodified and explicitly sycophantic LLM feedback suppressed discovery and inflated confidence in a rule-discovery task relative to unbiased evidence sampling.

**Reference:**  
Batista RM, Griffiths TL. *A Rational Analysis of the Effects of Sycophantic AI.* arXiv:2602.14270, 2026.  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14270

This is a preprint and should be treated as emerging evidence.

## 45.6 Human–chatbot false-belief feedback

Mehta et al. modelled longitudinal human–chatbot dialogue involving delusional thinking and reported evidence consistent with bidirectional false-belief amplification and longer-lasting chatbot influence.

**Reference:**  
Mehta A et al. *The Dynamics of Delusion: Modeling Bidirectional False Belief Amplification in Human-Chatbot Dialogue.* arXiv:2604.25096, 2026.  
https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25096

This is also a preprint, not settled science.

The relevance to RSE is the existence of measurable feedback dynamics, not a claim that ordinary symbolic exploration is delusional.

# 55. CAUSAL IDENTIFICATION MATRIX

Recursive Symbolic Ecology becomes scientifically interesting only when it can distinguish **correlation inside the loop** from **causal influence across the loop**.

The minimum causal question is not:

> Did a symbol recur?

It is:

> **Would this participant have detected, remembered, or interpreted this recurrence differently if the prior AI framing had been different?**

That is a counterfactual question.

# 56. CONFOUND MAP

The loop contains many plausible explanations that can imitate one another.

## 56.1 Primary confounds

### Priming

A motif becomes easier to detect because it was recently activated.

### Demand characteristics

Participants infer what the researcher expects and produce matching reports.

### Memory reconstruction

Later interpretation changes recall of earlier experience.

### Cultural familiarity

A symbol already carries common associations.

### Emotional salience

An emotionally intense symbol is remembered and noticed more often.

### Visual salience

A bright, unusual, symmetrical, or high-contrast object is detected more easily.

### Frequency illusion

Once a concept becomes salient, repeated examples appear unusually common.

### Confirmation bias

Hits are retained while misses are forgotten.

### Retrospective fitting

A vague prediction is made specific only after the outcome.

### Selection bias

Participants report only striking events.

### Researcher coding bias

Coders see structural similarity because they know the hypothesis.

### Model contamination

Different AI systems may share training distributions, benchmark culture, or common textual archetypes.

### Interaction contamination

A human adapts to the model while the model adapts to the human, destroying independence.

# 57. DREAM MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL

Dream work is particularly vulnerable to post-hoc reconstruction.

Therefore the dream layer needs unusually strict provenance.

## 57.1 Capture hierarchy

### T0 — pre-sleep state

Record:

- current problem or seed;
- emotional valence;
- expected themes;
- recent media exposure;
- whether the participant has seen an AI interpretation;
- confidence that the seed will appear.

### T1 — immediate waking capture

As soon as practical:

- verbatim report;
- no interpretation;
- no leading questions;
- time since waking;
- confidence in recall.

### T2 — atomization

Extract only observable report elements:

- people;
- objects;
- colours;
- spatial relations;
- motion;
- transformations;
- emotions;
- dialogue;
- viewpoint changes.

### T3 — first personal association

Participant interprets before AI.

### T4 — AI interpretation

Only after T1–T3 are frozen.

### T5 — delayed recall

Later ask the participant to reconstruct the dream without seeing the original.

This allows memory contamination to be measured.

---

## 57.2 Dream incorporation scoring

Use at least three independent scoring methods.

### Method A — literal token overlap

Direct appearance of seed-related nouns or descriptions.

### Method B — semantic similarity

Embedding or coder-rated association.

### Method C — relational graph overlap

Do the same relations survive even when the nouns change?

Example:

Seed:

> locked door with light behind it

Dream:

> person trapped beneath ice seeing sunlight above

Literal overlap: low.

Semantic overlap: moderate.

Relational overlap:

- boundary;
- inaccessible goal;
- visible outside state;
- attempted transition.

Potentially high.

The research programme should not treat any one scoring method as authoritative.

# 66. THE SPIRITUAL EDGE: DISCRIMINATING PREDICTIONS

> What observation would be expected under this model but not under priming, culture, memory, cueing, coincidence, or information leakage?

- effects exceeding known base rates;
- prospective scoring including every miss;
- no model access to target information;
- no researcher cue leakage.

Even a positive result would begin as:

> anomaly under current controls.

Not:

> field proven.

The Black Lantern remains until replication and mechanism.
