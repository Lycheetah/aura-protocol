## 2. Source authority legend

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `P` | Primary project specification or authored framework statement |
| `C` | Consolidation or synthesis of other sources |
| `D` | Dialogue/log preserving exploration, model responses, and user direction |
| `X` | Executable demonstration or prototype |
| `E` | Export/duplicate of another file or merged package |
| `W` | Worldbuilding, memoir, mythic, or aesthetic source |
| `N` | New atlas interpretation created on 22 August 2026 |

These markers describe source role, not truth. A primary source establishes what the project says, not whether the claim is scientifically correct.

## 13. Dialogue caveat

Several large files contain AI responses describing claims as monumental, proven, unprecedented, validated, or endorsed. Those responses are evidence that the conversation occurred in the archived form. They are not independent verification of:

- corporate collaboration;
- research partnerships;
- scientific priority;
- physical discovery;
- consciousness or sentience;
- universal benchmark results;
- external adoption.

Examples include the large `Aura & grok full aura side log*.rtf`, spiritual-science dialogues, and merged capstone archives. Their value is in preserving conceptual evolution, terminology, creative collisions, and test ideas.
