# AURA Protocol — Official Origin Archive

This repository is the **official raw-origin archive for the AURA Protocol and
its associated research** by Mackenzie Conor James Clark.

It is intentionally mixed: constitutional AI design, LAMAGUE, CASCADE, dream
and symbolic research, speculative science, spiritual/philosophical inquiry,
mythic worldbuilding, experiments, code, historical dialogue, and unfinished
ideas coexist here because that is the actual lineage.

## Read this correctly

This archive is an origin point, not a polished scientific publication and not
a claim that the total corpus can be independently verified. No reader, other
AI, institution, or model is being asked to certify the archive in its entirety.
Individual claims may be studied, challenged, built upon, or left unresolved;
the archive does not grant them authority merely because they are vivid,
mathematical, implemented, or repeated.

The right relationship to this work is:

> Preserve the raw idea. Name what kind of thing it is. Follow its provenance.
> Test what can be tested. Keep mystery and uncertainty visible.

Others are welcome to take influence from the work. Influence is not
endorsement, verification, or proof of the original claim.

## Status language

Documents use labels such as `[OBSERVED]`, `[HISTORICAL]`, `[AUTHORED]`,
`[BUILD]`, `[TESTABLE]`, `[HYPOTHESIS]`, `[SPECULATIVE]`, `[SYMBOLIC]`,
`[PERSONAL EXPERIENCE]`, `[WORLDBUILDING]`, and `[UNKNOWN]`. These labels
separate kinds of value; they do not form a claim that the entire archive is
verified.

## Preservation and cleanup

Raw originals, contradictions, failed ideas, and historical voice are retained.
Exact duplicate cleanup may remove redundant paths from the navigable surface,
but only after a checksum-based map and a reversible Git receipt. No readable or
formal rewrite may overwrite an original. Paired rewrites belong in a separate
revision lane with source anchors and verification sheets.

The archive's governing rule is:

> **No compression without recovery.**
